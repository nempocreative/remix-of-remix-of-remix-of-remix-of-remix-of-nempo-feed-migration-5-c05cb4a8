# Rules for `src/apps/`

- Sub-apps live in `src/apps/<key>/` (own `@/apps/<key>/` imports, `entry.tsx`, public files under `public/<base>/`) and mount at `/<base>` via `src/routes/<base>.index.tsx` + `<base>.$.tsx` using `AppShell`; why: each was a separate Vite app and they must not collide.
- GGS slide 12 uses `GgsWeAreOpenSlide`, scoped `ggo-` styles, welcoming image-to-video footage, original header/footer and pause/replay controls; its MP4 captures video seeks and CSS time steps from the same artboard, so download and preview match without modifying slide 11.
- GGS slides 13–18 share `GgsServicesSlide` (scoped `ggsv-` styles, `dense` variant for full slides): one service category per slide, names only — never prices — with the original header/footer overlay and a prebuilt PNG download per slide (regenerate all six from the preview at 1232 CSS px × 3, RGB, whenever the shared layout changes); why: the owner asked for the price list's service names spread across slides without prices.
- GGS slides 12–18 use the owner's newer header/footer overlay (`ggs-header-footer-v2.png.asset.json`); slides 7–11 keep the original `ggs-header-footer.png.asset.json`; why: the owner swapped the overlay only from slide 12 onward. Changing either overlay means re-recording slide 12's MP4 (1080×1440, 30 fps, 8 s, time-stepped video seek + animations) and remaking the 13–18 PNGs.
- GGS slide 19 is the open-loker (Eyelash Extension & Nail Art) slide `GgsLokerSlide` (scoped `ggl-` styles, colors in global CSS, v2 header/footer overlay, edited team portrait imported independently, no kicker text) with a prebuilt 3240×4320 RGB PNG download (`ggs-feed-19-loker.png`) captured from the preview at 1080 CSS px × 3; regenerate it whenever the slide changes; why: download must match the recruitment slide without modifying other slides.
- GGS Story slide 04 is the reels cover `GgsStoryCoverSlide` (scoped `ggc-` styles, colors in global CSS, 9:16; its own retouched photo shifted up so the centered headline sits below the subject without covering it) using its own story header/footer overlay (`ggs-story-header-footer.png.asset.json`) and downloads a pointer-only prebuilt 3240×5760 RGB PNG (`ggs-story-04-peduli-penampilan.png.asset.json`) through `PrebuiltPngDownload`, captured from the preview at 1080 CSS px × 3; regenerate it whenever the slide changes; why: the live in-browser export dropped the photo on iPhone/iPad, and the file is too close to the repo's 10 MB limit to commit.
- GGS Story slide 05 reuses `GgsStoryCoverSlide` with the `centered` prop (photo uncropped and centered, headline kept inside the middle 1080×1440 band so the story doubles as an Instagram feed cover crop) and its own pointer-only prebuilt 3240×5760 PNG (`ggs-story-05-peduli-penampilan-feed.png.asset.json`), captured the same way as slide 04; why: the owner wanted slide 04's design reusable as a 1080×1440 feed cover without changing slide 04.
